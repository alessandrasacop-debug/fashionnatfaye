Inside the Studio

The Atelier Process

Every Ferrero Design garment moves deliberately through three studio stages—honoring slow construction, zero-waste geometry, and tactile longevity.

01 Stage One

Concept & Sketching

Each silhouette begins with gouache and ink proportion studies balancing fluid evening movement against crisp streetwear structure. Zero-waste jigsaw pattern blocks are drafted before a single meter is cut.

Zero-Waste Blocks Gouache & Ink
02 Stage Two

Draping & Textiles

We pin unbleached organic silks, hemp twills, and botanical-dyed cloths directly on the dress form—testing bias tension, pleat depth, and light refraction in soft rose (#F9E4EC) and mint green (#E4F1EA).

Moulage on Stand Botanical Pigments
03 Stage Three

Final Construction

Master tailors and couture finishers assemble each piece in small numbered runs using French seams, hand-basted linings, and biodegradable trimmings—built to endure across decades and seasons.

French Seams Small-Batch Finish

Designer Bio & Philosophy

Quiet Luxury Rooted in Living Materials

Founded by designer Camila Ferrero, FERRERO DESIGN bridges high-drama evening couture with the effortless posture of modern streetwear. Every garment treats sustainability not as a constraint, but as a sculptural catalyst.

By pairing delicate petal hues (#F9E4EC) and botanical greens (#E4F1EA) with stark architectural white (#FFFFFF), the studio creates pieces that feel luminous in daylight and commanding after dark—crafted in partnership with regenerative fiber cooperatives and artisan ateliers.
